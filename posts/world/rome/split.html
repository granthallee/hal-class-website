1. The Roman Divorce - East vs West

Imagine you have a chocolate bar as big as your desk. It is your bar but the whole class is trying to eat it instead. What would you do?

As a table group, come up with three different solutions and write them in your notebook.

This was the situation in Rome around 300CE the empire was too big and it had too many enemies, the solution was to split it up into two regions each with their own empires, each with their own emperor and helper. This worked for 100 years until the two just became separate Empires, the ERE and WRE.
