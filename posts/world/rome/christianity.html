Religion in the classroom?

Imagine that one of your classmates said one of these things in class. Would it be an academic way to talk about religion?

Answer if each of these is an academic way to talk about religion or not

  1. "Well im a Christian so I know it's true, plus, my pastor told me that other religions are fake."
  2. "Even if it's an opinion about whether or not Jesus came back from the dead, it's a fact that Christians believe that."
  3. "Well I am Muslim and we believe this, that's different from what Christians believe."
  4. "Well Mr Hallee said that Christians think that Jesus is God, that means that he's saying my religion is wrong because we don't think that."

Write in your notes: Rules for talking about religion in class

  1. Be respectful of other people's opinions.
  2. Think about how other people might have a different opinion than you.
  3. Phrase beliefs as beliefs. IE "In my church we believe that..." not "It is true that..."
