What was the Songhai Empire like?

1. The Songhai Empire

  • Powerful warrior kings from the city of Gao.
  • Previously vassals of the Mali Empire
  • When Mali became weaker they declared independence and they eventually conquered Mali itself.
  • Lasted into the early modern period before they were destroyed by the Moroccans and their guns.
