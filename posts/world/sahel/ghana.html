1. The Empire of Ghana

  • West African Empire
  • Absolute monarchy - meaning ruled by a king (kaya).
  • The king had vassals - other lords and kings who accepted his rule.
  • Very rich and powerful because of control over gold mines.
  • Eventually adopted Islam as a religion originally were polytheist

2. Spread of Islam to West Africa

  • Islam started in the middle east and first spread through the expansion of the caliphate.
  • Then it spread again through trade routes.
  • While the kingdom of Ghana officially had its own religion, the elite (most powerful people) were largely Muslim
  • This led to West Africa becoming Muslim over time.
